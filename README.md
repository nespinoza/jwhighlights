# Figures from "Highlights from Exoplanet Observations by the James Webb Space Telescope" (Espinoza & Perrin, 2025)
-------------------------------

This repository holds Python scripts and Inkscape-compatible `*.svg` (i.e., vectorized) files to reproduce the figures in 
our ["Highlights from Exoplanet Observations by the James Webb Space Telescope" (Espinoza & Perrin, 2025)](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2025arXiv250520520E/abstract) review chapter that will be part of the Handbook of Exoplanets, 2nd Edition (Hans Deeg and Juan Antonio Belmonte Eds. in Chief).

If you make use of these figures for talks, proposals, or your own reviews --- please do cite the Chapter.

If you find any issues, please feel free to open a [Github Issue](https://github.com/nespinoza/jwhighlights/issues).
